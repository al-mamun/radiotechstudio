These Are the Radio Problems
We Fix Every Day

Every issue below has a clear technical solution. We diagnose fast and fix it right — the first time.

🔁

Playlists Not Switching

Your automation software loops the same playlist instead of following the configured schedule or rotation rules.

🔔

Hourly Jingles Looping

Jingles fire repeatedly or play at the wrong times due to misconfigured event triggers or category rules.

🏷️

Metadata Not Showing

Song titles and artist names aren't appearing in your website player, mobile app, or radio directories.

📡

Stream Disconnecting

Your stream drops unexpectedly, leaving listeners with silence or a connection error during live broadcasts.

🤖

AutoDJ Not Working

The AutoDJ fails to start, crashes mid-session, or plays silence instead of transitioning to your music library.

🌐

Website Player Issues

Your embedded radio player doesn't load, buffers endlessly, shows the wrong song, or breaks on mobile browsers.

🎙️

Live DJ Connection Errors

DJs can't connect to the broadcast source or keep getting disconnected mid-show, disrupting the live feed.

📹

YouTube / Facebook Stream Stops

Your 24/7 visual radio stream cuts out, fails to restart, or loses sync on social streaming platforms.

Also fixing: Scheduler conflicts · Wrong playlist timing · Double jingle play · SSL stream failures · VPS server errors · AzuraCast Docker issues · Mountpoint problems
